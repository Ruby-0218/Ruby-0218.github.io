Hi, I’m Ju-Ting Sun, a Master’s student in the Quantitative Methods in the Social Sciences (Data Science Track) program at Columbia University. I specialize in leveraging machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), and UI/UX design to transform unstructured data into actionable solutions. My current focus bridges technical data engineering with real-world applications in sustainability and healthcare.

In the industry, I have been actively building impactful AI and SaaS products. Most recently, I was a Machine Learning Intern at NociQuant, a biotech startup, where I engineered a clinical text extraction pipeline. Using the Gemini API and machine learning frameworks like PyTorch and Hugging Face, I helped parse unstructured electronic health records into temporal pattern classifiers for complex diagnostics.

I am also the Co-Founder and COO of Carbon Walker, a technology startup. We launched an automated transportation carbon-tracking SaaS designed to meet ISO 14064 and CBAM compliance. By validating UX usability and devising Python-based compliance estimation modules, the platform optimizes emission monitoring for both individuals and enterprises (I gotta say founding a startup is ABSOLUTELY not easy). Previously, I led a technical project for IBM, optimizing generative AI workflows and resolving critical system bottlenecks.

I soon realized that tackling broader issues like sustainable development goals and carbon rights required robust quantitative tools, which drove my pivot into data science.
