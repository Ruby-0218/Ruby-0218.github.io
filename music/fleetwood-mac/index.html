Fun fact

I’ve never been through anything like this myself, but I’ve always felt that tragic love stories have a strange kind of magic. They hurt, sure, but they also leave behind some of the best music ever made.

This song is a proof. Stevie Nicks wrote it after spotting a highway sign reading “Silver Spring, Maryland” while going through a messy breakup with her bandmate, Lindsey Buckingham. To her, it represented a dreamland where their love actually worked out.

The irony? The band cut the track from their iconic album Rumours and buried it on the B-side of “Go Your Own Way” - Lindsey’s own diss track about her. But Stevie left a quiet little promise in her lyrics:

“You’ll never get away from the sound of the woman that loved you.”

Fast forward 20 years to their 1997 reunion concert. Mid-song, Stevie turned around, looked Lindsey dead in the eye, and sang those exact words straight to his face while he had to keep playing guitar and singing harmony. Talk about an elite level of pettiness.

Lesson learned: If you’re going to break up, maybe don’t do it with someone holding the microphone ;)
