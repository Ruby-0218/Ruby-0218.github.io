Michael Jackson

The performer who made pop feel cinematic

1964–2009 Gary, Indiana, United States Pop · R&B · Soul · Funk

Michael Jackson is one of those artists whose songs feel inseparable from performance. Even when I am only listening, the choreography, visual storytelling, and atmosphere of the music still seem present.

Michael Jackson is one of the artists I return to when I want pop music that feels carefully built around rhythm, movement, drama, and visual storytelling. Honestly, my only regret is that I didn’t dive into his world sooner. It wasn’t until I watched the Broadway show MJ the Musical and his documentary film that I truly fell in love with his artistry and started listening to all of his songs on repeat. He is a 100% absolute genius. Without a single doubt.

Fun fact

There’s something undeniably cool about turning a severe injury into the ultimate flex. The music video for this song was already an absolute spectacle featuring Eddie Murphy, Iman, and Magic Johnson… but the real magic happened live.

At the 1993 Soul Train Music Awards, MJ was supposed to perform this insanely complex choreography. The problem? He had a severely twisted ankle. So instead of canceling, he performed the entire routine while sitting down in an ornate chair. And somehow, he still grooved harder and looked cooler than anyone else in the room standing on two feet. (I mean, he’s MJ, so not surprised)

Lesson learned: If you’re a true king, you don’t even need to stand up to steal the show ;)

Lesson learned: Never assume a princess doesn’t want to rock out to a song about scandalous groupies ;)

I guess sometimes making art requires a level of street cred that no politician can understand.

I’ve always felt that tragic missed opportunities in music history are fascinating.

This track was originally supposed to be the ultimate pop-royalty showdown: a duet between Michael Jackson and Prince! MJ sent Prince the demo, hoping for an epic face-off. But Prince heard the opening lyric (“Your butt is mine”) and instantly rejected it.

Prince famously joked in interviews, “Who is singing that to whom? Because you sure aren’t singing it to me!” So MJ just went ahead and made it a solo masterpiece instead. (Gosh I really want to see them work together…)

Lesson learned: Be careful with your opening lyrics if you’re trying to collaborate with another massive ego ;)

I always thought “Annie” was some mysterious woman caught in a dark underworld, but the truth is way more clinical.

Ever wondered why MJ keeps aggressively asking, “Annie, are you OK?” It’s actually a direct reference to “Resusci Anne” - the standard mannequin used in CPR training classes worldwide! MJ had recently taken a CPR class, where you are literally taught to shake the dummy and ask that exact question.

And guess what? The phrase just stuck in his head, turning standard first-aid protocol into music history, I have to say, well-done, never thought this could be a song… really a genius.

Lesson learned: Pay attention in your first-aid classes, even though you can’t make a song, at least you can save someone ;)

Imagine a world where the ultimate Halloween anthem was actually just a sparkly disco track.
